Rocket! – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Rocket! (Android package io.github.vscdev90.rocket) uses and why. Questions: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

No account, no personal data from us

You do not need an account to play. We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information about you. Your game progress (coins, upgrades, records, settings) is stored only on your own device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded videos: you can choose to watch a short video ad in exchange for an in-game reward. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and explains its use of data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Watching ads is always optional; you never have to watch an ad to play.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game: 🏆 Collection → Privacy & ad settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

Other features

Sharing a score picture uses your phone's own share menu; nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with. The countdown voice uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech. Vibration is used for haptic feedback. None of these send information to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
